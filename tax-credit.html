How to find real, current incentives

Start with the U.S. Department of Energy's Alternative Fuels Data Center laws & incentives database, then open the official page for each program it lists. Check your electric utility next: many offer rebates for home chargers and cheaper time-of-use rates for overnight charging. Finally, ask the dealer which offers are current and read the terms — including income limits, price caps and application deadlines.

Remember that incentives are only one input. Fuel, maintenance, insurance and depreciation usually decide the true cost — see our 5-year cost calculator and break-even calculator.
