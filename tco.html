How the 5-year cost of ownership calculator works

What it adds up

Total cost of ownership = purchase price + energy + insurance + maintenance + fees − expected resale value − any incentives, over five years. Depreciation (the difference between price and resale value) is usually the largest single cost.

Getting accurate inputs

Get real insurance quotes for both cars, use your actual electricity and gas prices, and check recent used prices for resale value. Federal EV credits ended for vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025 — enter only state or utility incentives you have verified.

Limits

The tool uses fixed prices over the period and does not model interest, taxes on incentives or major repairs. Read our worked example in True Cost of Owning an EV in 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include depreciation?

Yes, through the purchase price and the resale value you enter for each car.

Which costs matter most?

For most people it is depreciation, followed by energy and insurance. Maintenance is usually smaller.
