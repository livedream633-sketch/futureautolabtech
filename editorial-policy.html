Trust & Transparency

Editorial Policy & Advertising Disclosure

FutureAutoLab publishes news, guides and free calculators about electric vehicles. This page explains how we work, how we handle mistakes and how we make money, so you can decide how much to trust what you read here.

Our standards

  • →Accuracy first: we check prices, specifications, rules and dates against manufacturer pages, government sources and other primary sources before publishing.
  • →Clear dates: guides show when they were last updated. If a fact is time-sensitive (prices, incentives, regulations) we say so and link to the official source.
  • →No invented news: we do not publish unverified rumours as fact. Live news feeds on our site are automatically aggregated headlines linking to the original publishers and are not written by us.
  • →Independence: advertisers and partners do not review or influence our editorial content.

Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help with research, outlining and editing. Anything we publish is reviewed by a person for accuracy before it goes live, and AI tools are not treated as a source of facts.

Corrections

If you find an error, please tell us through the contact form. We will review it promptly, correct verified errors and note significant corrections on the page.

Advertising and affiliate disclosure

FutureAutoLab is supported by advertising, including Google AdSense. Ads are labelled and are separate from editorial content. If we ever use affiliate links, we will disclose it on the page. See also our Disclaimer and Privacy Policy.

Calculators

Our tools use simplified models and the inputs you enter. Results are estimates for planning, not financial, tax or legal advice. Each tool explains its assumptions on its page.
